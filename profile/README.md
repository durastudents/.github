## 🌟 About Us

The **Dura Students Society** is an organization dedicated to connecting Dura youth, strengthening community identity, preserving the indigenous Dura language and culture, and creating sustainable opportunities for future generations. 

Beyond cultural preservation, we serve as a collaborative technology hub supporting education, computer science learning, modern web/mobile development, and community-driven digital archiving.

---

## 🏛️ The Dura Digital Heritage Project

We are building a living, community-owned digital heritage platform to document, preserve, and revitalize Dura history and culture through interconnected collections:

* **🗣️ Dura Language (Dura Lopa):** Vocabulary, pronunciation guides, audio recordings, example sentences, and interactive learning apps.
* **📜 Oral History:** Recorded memories and knowledge shared by elders, knowledge holders, and cultural practitioners.
* **📍 Places & Maps:** Historical settlements, traditional place names, meanings, and geographical maps of where Dura people live in Nepal and abroad.
* **👥 People (Dura Peto):** Notable figures and community members who preserved and passed on cultural knowledge.
* **🎭 Living Culture:** Festivals, rituals, traditional music, songs, dances, crafts, and ancestral practices.
* **📸 Archives & Documents:** Historical photographs, manuscripts, research papers, certificates, audio, and video collections.

---

## 🚀 Technology & Open Source

We blend indigenous heritage preservation with modern software engineering and educational tools:
* **📱 Modern App & Web Development:** Cross-platform mobile applications (Flutter) and web architectures (Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS) powering community archives and digital language tools.
* **💻 Educational Worksheets & CS Curricula:** Practical coding exercises, lesson guides, and resources designed for secondary school computer science and technology education.
* **📊 Data & Digital Archiving:** Structured metadata pipelines, taxonomies, and Python-based data workflows for managing historical collections.
